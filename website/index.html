The Problem
Session music shouldn't fight you

Every DM knows the struggle. You've curated the perfect playlists — but actually using them mid-session is a nightmare.

🔀
Jarring switches

Spotify and YouTube don't crossfade between playlists. Combat music cuts to tavern music with an ugly silence gap or hard switch.

💻
Tab juggling

Alt-tabbing to a browser while your players watch you fumble for the right playlist. Mood: killed. Immersion: shattered.

🧠
Cognitive overhead

You're already managing NPCs, combat, and story. Remembering which playlist goes with which scene is one thing too many.

How It Works
Three steps to atmospheric sessions
01
Build your soundscape

Create a campaign — or start with the 15 pre-built RPG moods. Add climates like combat, tavern, forest, and mystery, each with its own playlist of YouTube or local tracks, plus optional ambient layers underneath.

02
Scan the QR code

Ambora starts a local server on your network. Scan the QR with your phone — no app install, no account. Your phone becomes a remote control.

03
Tap to set the mood

One tap switches climates with a smooth crossfade — music and ambience together. Your players hear the tavern chatter melt into forest wind. Then you tap Raven, and nobody knows why they're nervous.

Features
Everything a DM needs. Nothing more.

Ambora focuses on doing one thing exceptionally well — letting you shape the sound of a scene without breaking your flow.

🎨
Color-coded climates

Each climate gets its own color and icon — choose from 16 colors and 20 icons. On the phone, they're large, glowing cards you can identify at a glance — even in a dimly lit room.

🔊
Seamless crossfading

Dual-channel audio engine with configurable crossfade (1–10 seconds). Old music fades out while new music fades in. No gaps, no silence.

🌬️
Ambient sound layers

A forest isn't one looping file. Stack layers under the music: wind looping continuously, birds every 8–20 seconds, a branch cracking every half minute, and a raven only when you tap it. Each layer has its own volume, and random layers never overlap themselves — the next delay starts when the last clip ends.

📱
Phone remote

Your phone becomes the remote via local WiFi. No app to install — just scan a QR code. Switch scenes, ride the volume, toggle ambient layers, and fire one-shots from big tap-friendly pads.

▶️
YouTube + local files

Paste YouTube URLs or use your own MP3, WAV, OGG, or FLAC files. Mix both in the same climate playlist.

🎚️
Volume normalization

LUFS-based auto-leveling analyses your local tracks and holds them all at the same perceived loudness — no more diving for the volume knob between songs.

🔇
Fade to silence

One button gently fades everything to silence for dramatic moments. Tap again to resume. Volume control always at your fingertips.

🗂️
Campaign organization

Organize music by campaign. Switch between The Lost Mines, Curse of Strahd, and your homebrew without losing your setup.

🌙
Dark by design

Built for dimly lit game rooms. The dark interface never glares at you or draws your players' attention to your screen.

📦
Export & share

Export campaigns as .ambora files and share them with other DMs. Import with one click — tracks, climates, and settings included.

🎲
Starter campaign

New to Ambora? Start with 15 pre-built RPG moods — combat, tavern, mystery, and more — ready to play out of the box.

🔀
Shuffle mode

Shuffle tracks within a climate so your playlist never feels predictable. Toggle per climate for variety across long sessions.

🛡️
Signed & notarized on macOS

macOS builds are signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so Ambora opens with a double-click — no “unidentified developer” warning, no right-click workaround. And it asks for no permissions it doesn’t need.

Open Source
Built in the open. Free forever.

Ambora is MIT-licensed. No subscriptions, no telemetry, no data collection. Your music stays on your machine.

🔓
Fully local

No cloud servers. Music plays from your computer. Phone connects via your local WiFi. Nothing leaves your network.

🛠️
Contribute

Built with Electron, React, TypeScript, and shadcn/ui. PRs welcome — check the CONTRIBUTING guide on GitHub.

🧩
Extensible

Clean architecture. Add new music sources, sound effects, or integrations. The codebase is documented and approachable.

🔒
Private by default

Zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero tracking. We don't know you use it. That's by design.
